# Halloween, explicitly optional

Halloween 2026 was a special edition. The recurring process now selects the saved Halloween design on the Wednesday on or immediately before October 31 each year; regular is used on other non-holiday Wednesdays. Keep the football facts and normal editorial/layout contract.

`2026/approved.html` preserves the original accepted page. `2026/issue.json` retains its narrative with later targeted proofreading fixes so reuse does not reintroduce them. `2026/brief.json` is its contemporaneous private fact brief; `2026/elo-source.json` retains the original source Elo document. Render fresh holiday drafts from their own validated week, not last year's stats. The original `newsletter/templates/halloween.css` remains a historical reference. New explicitly requested Halloween drafts use the reusable `redesign/` assets through `weekly.py render --out <draft> --theme halloween`; `redesign/week7-sample.html` shows the refreshed typography, palette, borders, shading, original margin art and subtle background image with the accepted 2026 writing. Published and archived 2026 files remain unchanged.

The recipe: build the opening around the week's actual surprise as an arrival at a haunted league manor. Carry manor-room, spectral-invitation, moonlight, candle and cobweb images through a few section transitions and award names. Each joke still lands on the named team's actual performance or decision; avoid replacing reporting with a pile of spooky fragments. Use original manor/moon/ghost/pumpkin/candle/mist/cobweb artwork and a playful Haunted-Mansion-level fright. Keep it friendly, with no gore and no Disney artwork or assets. Preserve text contrast, player grouping and mobile readability. The archived page is the exact visual/narrative reference, not a mandate to repeat the same plot or roster jokes next year.

For next year: obtain new data with that season's verified config/baseline, draft the regular story first, create a separate Halloween draft directory, apply the narrative/skin, then make sure the themed page is factually correct and readable. Publish through the normal scheduled workflow. Never update old approved bytes as part of a trial.
