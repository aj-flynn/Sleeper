# Scheduled holiday editions

The original regular design is the normal edition. AJ authorizes the saved Halloween, Thanksgiving and Christmas themes on the Wednesday on or immediately before the US holiday. `weekly.py render` defaults to `--theme auto` and resolves this by year. Halloween is October 31; Thanksgiving is the fourth Thursday of November; Christmas is December 25. Other holidays use regular. In 2026 the themed Wednesdays are October 28, November 25 and December 23. Week 7 on October 28 is already announced; never retro-post it.

Halloween's reusable assets are under `halloween/redesign/`; Thanksgiving and Christmas have their own directories. Keep all holiday artwork inline and the original newspaper layout intact. Use the saved writing guidance with fresh weekly data; Week 7 examples are historical examples, not future facts. Explicit `--theme regular` or another supported theme remains available for requested local comparisons.

No preview/process/approval area, wrapper, banner or reserved space belongs in output. Standouts continues directly to Injuries, with no bench prose beneath the table. Use the neutral narrator and individual-manager he/him/his rules in EDITORIAL.md. The rejected regular redesign candidate is not part of this process.
