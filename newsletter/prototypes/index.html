Five new designs for the newsletter

Each one is the same real Week 4 issue, so you’re comparing design and nothing else. Every number is from the league on Sleeper and the NFL box scores, with ESPN for game context. Open them on your phone first, since that’s where the league reads them.

These are drafts on a branch. None of them is published, and the live issues are unchanged.

  • Meeting Minutes

    The league is called Rebuilders Anonymous, so the issue is the typed handout from the weekly meeting, with one red pen circling the number that matters in each item.

    Courier Prime for everything typed, Kalam for the margin notes.

    Open Meeting Minutes

  • Blueprint

    Rebuilders, taken literally: the week as a construction drawing, where every margin of victory is drawn to scale as a dimension line between the two scores.

    Barlow Condensed lettering, Barlow text, Architects Daughter for the draftsman’s notes.

    Open Blueprint

  • The Docket

    A roast is a trial, so each matchup is filed as a case caption, the box score goes in as lettered exhibits, and numbered paragraphs let anyone in the chat cite paragraph 14.

    Libre Caslon Text throughout, set like a filed pleading.

    Open The Docket

  • Card Back

    The NFL half of every issue is a stat line, and the back of a football card is the most-read stat line ever printed, so each matchup is printed as one.

    Alfa Slab One for names and numbers, Archivo Narrow for the stat tables.

    Open Card Back

  • Downfield

    The standings are drawn on a football field, each team at the spot its season points earn, so the gap between first and last reads as distance.

    Saira Stencil One for painted numbers, Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading.

    Open Downfield

Where the numbers come from

The data brief for this issue, with every source URL and stat line, is in newsletter/issues/2026-week4/brief.md. The raw API responses are saved next to it.
