Item 1. Lead story

1 snap from the Superflex, and Micah still won by 29.58.

Superflex, technically

Micah won with a quarterback who took one snap

Case Keenum handed off once for the Bears and sat down. Micah parsons fan club won by 29.58 anyway, because the other ten starters had a week.

Taken down by The Rebuilders Anonymous desk.

Micah parsons fan club played Week 4 with ten working starters and won by 29.58. The eleventh was Case Keenum at Superflex. According to ESPN, Keenum lined up under center for Chicago’s first play against the Jets, pitched to D’Andre Swift for 2 yards, and then Tyson Bagent took over at quarterback for the rest of the game. Keenum’s line for the week is 1 snap and 0.0 points.

Keenum was a reasonable pickup. Micah added him on September 30, after he started Chicago’s Monday night win over Philadelphia and threw for 247 yards and two touchdowns, and started him in Week 4 with Baker Mayfield out with a dislocated thumb. To make room, Micah dropped Keon Coleman. Coleman caught 6 passes for 116 yards and a touchdown for Buffalo on Sunday, including a 34-yard score from Josh Allen.

The rest of the lineup made it a non-event. Javonte Williams ran for three touchdowns in Dallas’s 34-30 win in Houston, all in the second half and a career high, for 31.3. Hockenson caught every ball thrown to him in Minnesota’s 15-10 win over Miami (24.9). Zay Flowers had 8 catches for 118 yards and a touchdown (25.8), and Jake Bates made all four of his field goals for 18.0.

Even the other quarterback got hurt. Lamar Jackson left Baltimore’s win over Tennessee with an ankle injury after 33 of 64 snaps and still scored 18.88 on 15-of-20 passing for 222 yards and two touchdowns. Micah has since claimed Bagent for $0, dropped Keenum, and added Tyler Huntley, who went 8 of 9 in relief of Jackson. Micah is 3-1 with the sixth-most points in the league and $10 of FAAB left.
