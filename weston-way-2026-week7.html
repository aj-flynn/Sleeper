The short version

Allen This Together won by 4.45 with two bye-week zeros. Tony Pollard carried the candy while the ghosts took credit.

Allen This Together has won four straight, which is how a haunted house ends up getting good reviews despite two rooms being empty. The team started Josh Allen and the Jaguars defense on their NFL byes. Both scored zero. Tony Pollard responded with 129 rushing yards, three rushing touchdowns and 50.90 fantasy points, dragging the whole operation past Return of the Mac by 4.45. Pollard wore a running back costume and hauled the entire group through the corn maze while the quarterback admired the scenery.

Mac had its own collection of zeros and still came close enough to make this irritating. Drake Maye supplied 33.25, but the 139.20–134.75 result dropped Mac to 1–6. Allen’s fourth straight win came with the lowest winning score of Week 7. Mac followed the trail of candy all the way to the exit and discovered that Allen had locked it from the other side.

Meanwhile, St. Brown Sugar Baby Back Gibbs scored 192.80 and improved to 6–0, beating Rachel’s Remarkable Team by 71.85. Hail Mary returned from a bye with a win, ClearEyes won with a freshly acquired Tre Tucker, and Linder somehow won with three zero-point starters. The Weston Way has eleven houses on the trick-or-treat route, and apparently several can pass inspection with nobody answering half the doors.
